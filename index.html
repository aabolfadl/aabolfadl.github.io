Cents

Personal finance · 5.0 · Free

View on the App Store

A money app that fills itself in.

Cents keeps track of spending, budgets, savings goals, loans and gold for people in Egypt. Its party trick: when your bank texts you about a payment, the payment shows up in Cents on its own.

  • Records bank texts and Instapay transfers automatically
  • Budgets that reset on payday, not on the 1st of the month
  • Tracks Gam3eya circles, loans, gold and silver, in any currency

The clever part

It records a payment straight from the bank's text message without the app opening, even while the phone is locked. And there is no server behind it: people's money data never leaves their phone.

  • Home-screen widgets
  • Arabic & English
  • Face ID lock
  • Dark mode
  • Live exchange rates
  • Spreadsheet export

Lckdin

Health & fitness · 5.0 · Free

View on the App Store

A gym log built for the gym floor.

Lckdin lets lifters log every set in a couple of taps, then turns the numbers into charts, personal records and a heatmap of the muscles they've trained, and the ones they've been skipping.

  • 283 exercises with demo clips, and 6 ready-made training plans
  • Spots new personal records by itself after every workout
  • A front-and-back muscle heatmap, plus 72 free stats

The clever part

It works with no signal at all, basement gyms included. Workouts, body measurements and progress photos never leave the phone, yet it still syncs with Apple Health.

  • Apple Health
  • Progress photos
  • Rest-timer alerts
  • Face ID lock
  • Supersets & drop sets
  • Built-in ads

Pico

Puzzle games · 5.0 · Free

View on the App Store

18 new puzzles every morning. No server.

Pico gives players a crossword, a word game and a sudoku every day, each in several sizes and difficulty levels: 18 fresh puzzles a day, free.

  • Crosswords in five sizes, from a quick 5×5 to a full 15×15
  • Wordform: guess the word, from 4 to 11 letters
  • Sudoku from Easy to Evil, with notes that tidy themselves

The clever part

Nobody writes Pico's puzzles and no server sends them. The phone builds each one from the date, so everyone in the world gets the same puzzle on the same day, from a dictionary of 99,503 clues packed inside the app.

  • Streaks & stats
  • Daily reminders
  • High-contrast mode
  • Share your result
  • Works offline
  • Built-in ads

Trivial

Trivia game · 5.0 · Free

View on the App Store

Turns one phone into a game show.

Trivial is trivia for one player racing the clock, or for a whole room of friends passing a single phone around. Hosts can even build their own Jeopardy-style board.

  • 5,141 questions across 53 topics, all inside the app

The clever part

Players can mix any topics and difficulty levels they like, and the matching questions are ready instantly: the question bank is laid out so the app never has to search through it. It all works in airplane mode.

  • Party & team modes

Questions

Good to know before we start.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You describe what the app should do in your own words. I handle everything technical and explain the choices without jargon.

Will my app need a server or monthly fees?

Often it won't. All four of my apps run entirely on the phone, so the only running cost is Apple's yearly developer fee. If your app needs accounts or syncing between devices, I'll tell you up front what that costs.

Can it work on Android too?

Yes. I build with React Native, which runs on both iPhone and Android from one set of code, so adding Android later doesn't mean starting over.

Who owns the app when we're done?

You do: the code, the App Store listing and the designs.

How long does an app take?

It depends on what the app does. For a sense of pace, my four apps went live within six weeks of each other. Before we start, I'll give you a realistic timeline for yours.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. Bug fixes, new features, App Store screenshots and keywords, ads and in-app purchases: whatever helps the app grow.

Got an app idea? Let's build it.

Tell me what you have in mind. A rough idea is fine. I'll reply with how I'd build it, how long it would take and what it would cost.
